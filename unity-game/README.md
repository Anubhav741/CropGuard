# Jalrakshak — The Last Drop

A third-person Unity conservation campaign in a drought-stricken village. All scenery, characters, meshes, materials, UI, animations, particles and sound are generated by Unity C# and Unity components. No image-generated assets or external art tools are used. Game quantities are illustrative, not scientific forecasts.

## Play in Unity

Open this project in Unity 6000.5.10f1. Open `Assets/Scenes/Village.unity` and press Play. The scene bootstraps the procedural world at runtime. Enter begins the cinematic introduction.

- WASD or arrows: move relative to the camera. Mouse: look.
- Shift: run. Space: jump.
- Approach a coloured station, then E: interact. The first pond encounter opens automatically.
- Number keys or buttons: operate the nearby equipment or choose its policy.
- E: close interaction. Esc: close or pause. Tab: village map and water ledger.

Interactions, pause and ledger stop the simulation. The mouse unlocks for these panels. In the Editor, click the Game view to return keyboard focus after using an Editor menu.

## Campaign

Explore the village square, pond, farmland, seed shed, hand pump, homes, river drain, recharge ground, storage tank, school, animal shelter, forest belt and distribution centre. Meera and the villagers explain the local situation.

Reaching the pond begins the weather timeline. Open its inlet before the rain: runoff flows into the pond and its visible surface rises. Closing it loses runoff. Two 210-second seasonal cycles combine rainfall, storms and dry periods. Continuous demand, leaks, evaporation, irrigation and pumping change the water budget.

Choose drip, sprinkler, flood or groundwater irrigation, or stop irrigation. Diversify to millet and return to harvest mature crops. Repair leaks, recharge groundwater, collect roof runoff, store overflow and release reserves. Plant and water trees, connect the school and animal shelter, share water fairly, clear contamination, add drainage and protect homes from flooding. Coins constrain upgrades; harvests finance further improvements. One-time achievements cannot be repeatedly farmed for points.

Water, soil, crops, food, happiness, prosperity, population, biodiversity, water quality, flood risk and sector health affect outcomes. Pond size, growing crops, trees, houses, river flow, rain, pump motion and school/animal conditions visibly respond. The camera follows the animated player and avoids solid obstacles. Weather includes procedural rain/thunder audio and lightning.

Survive both cycles with at least two harvests, food, safe water, adequate groundwater and healthy villagers, school and animals. Sustained shortages or collapse can end the campaign early. The ending explains the limiting resource; replay starts a fresh session.

## Unity validation and Web target

**Jalrakshak > Run gameplay simulation checks** runs checks inside Unity Editor: conservation victory, uncontrolled extraction loss, rain triggered by pond arrival, dry irrigation rejection, drip/flood tradeoffs, one-time rewards, frame-step consistency and pause.

**Jalrakshak > Build WebGL** builds the same Unity scene using installed Web Build Support. Runtime primitives are retained by `Assets/link.xml`; the colour shader is explicitly included in Resources. Its WebGL build also completed successfully; this revision has not had a browser runtime playthrough. Do not confuse older generated Web builds with this source revision.

## Scope

This is the Unity gameplay portion. Registration, authenticated online scores, certificates and backend integration remain separate application work. Scores currently belong to the running session; there is no claim of online persistence or a resume system. It is a stylized procedural village campaign, not photorealistic production art.
